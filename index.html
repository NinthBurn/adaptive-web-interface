The means by which interaction or communication is achieved.

Chapter / section 01 — Understanding

Here's the brief on this concept.

An interface is where different things meet, and where that meeting becomes useful. No need to scratch your head.

Definition

The place at which independent and often unrelated systems meet and act on, communicate with, or affect each other.

inter + face

PREFIX

between or among

Shows a relationship or connection between two or more things.

NOUN

a front, upper, or outer surface

As a verb, it also means to cover the front or surface of something.

01

Interface as a boundary

A surface or threshold where two different environments, materials, or domains meet.

air / water · land / sea
02

Interface as a point of interaction

A place through which one thing can act upon or respond to another.

door handle · control panel
03

Interface in computing

A defined way for a person, program, device, or system to communicate with another.

GUI · API · command line
04

Interface as a contract

A set of expectations that lets independent parts work together without knowing everything about each other.

protocol · software interface
05

Biological interface

A boundary through which organisms, cells, or biological systems exchange matter, energy, or signals.

cell membrane · tissue boundary
06

Interface as translation

A medium that makes one system understandable or usable to another, transforming information along the way.

language · symbols · protocols

Chapter / section 02 — Classification

Types of interfaces.

The word interface appears in many different fields. Its purpose changes, but the underlying idea remains: creating a point of connection between separate things.

01

Human ↔ Machine

User Interface (UI)

The visual, audio, and physical elements that allow a person to interact with a computer, machine, or application.

Screens · Buttons · Voice commands
02

Device ↔ Device

Hardware Interface

The physical connectors, electrical signals, and communication protocols that allow electronic devices to connect and exchange data.

USB · HDMI · PCI Express
03

Software ↔ Software

Application Programming Interface (API)

A defined set of rules, methods, and protocols that allows software systems to communicate without exposing their internal implementation.

Web APIs · Libraries · Endpoints
04

Class ↔ Contract

Object-Oriented Interface

A programming contract that specifies which methods a class must provide, allowing different classes to be used through a shared structure.

Interfaces · Abstract Classes · Polymorphism
05

Material ↔ Material

Physical Interface

The boundary where two distinct materials, phases, or environments meet and interact.

Oil and water · Cell membranes · Air and liquid

Chapter / section 03 — Word Formation

Forms & derivatives

The word interface takes on different forms depending on how it is used. Explore its variations and the meanings they carry.

Noun / Verb

interface

Explore form ↗

interface

A point where two systems, people, or things meet and interact. As a verb, it means to connect or interact with something.

Example: The application interfaces with the database.

Plural noun

interfaces

Explore form ↗

interfaces

The plural form of interface, referring to multiple points of contact or interaction between systems, people, or environments.

Example: The interfaces between the different systems need to be consistent.

Verb

interfacing

Explore form ↗

interfacing

The process or act of connecting and interacting with another system, person, or component. Also the present participle of interface.

Example: Interfacing with the client requires a great deal of patience.

Past tense

interfaced

Explore form ↗

interfaced

The past tense and past participle of interface, describing systems or components that were connected or brought into interaction.

Example: The two systems interfaced successfully during the test.

Adjective

interfacial

Explore form ↗

interfacial

Relating to an interface, especially the boundary or contact surface between two distinct materials, phases, or systems.

Example: Scientists are measuring the interfacial tension between the oil and water layers.

Adverb

interfacially

Explore form ↗

interfacially

In a manner that relates to an interface (rarely used).

Example: The two distinct chemical layers reacted interfacially, creating a thin film at the boundary.
